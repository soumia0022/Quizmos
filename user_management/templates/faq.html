Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

How do Polls work?

Polls are interactive questions that can be answered in real-time. Participants can submit their responses, and results are displayed instantly.

Is the platform free?

It is completley free to use.

Do Polls work on all devices?

Polls are designed to be responsive and work seamlessly across all devices, including smartphones, tablets, and desktops via the web browser.

What should I do if I forget my account password?

If you forget your password, you can use the 'Forgot Password' link on the login page to reset it. Follow the instructions sent to your email.

How secure is my data?

We take data security seriously. We never share your personal information with third parties.

What types of questions are there?

We support multiple question types including multiple choice, drag and drop, matching and word clouds.

Student Questions

How do I join a Poll?

Enter the Poll code or scan the QR code shared with you.

Do I need an account to participate?

No, you can participate as a guest using just the quiz code. However, creating an account helps track your progress.

Can I participate in a Poll at any time?

No, you can only participate when the Poll has been started by the lecturer.

How do I access Polls on my mobile device?

You can access Polls via the web browser on your mobile device. Just enter the Poll code or scan the QR code.

Can I see my past Poll results?

Yes, if you have an account, all your Poll attempts and scores are saved in your dashboard.

Lecturer Questions

How do I create a Poll?

Log in to your account, and click the 'Create Poll' button You can then select the type of questions you want and add them to your Poll.

Can I reuse my Polls?

Yes, all your Polls are saved in your dashboard and can be reused.

How do I share my Poll with students?

After creating your Poll, you will have the option to start the Poll. Once the Poll is started, you can display a number and QR code that students can use to join.

Can I export Poll results?

Yes, you can export the results to a CSV file for further analysis or record-keeping.

Can I embed media into the questions

Yes, you can add video, audio and pictures into your questions.

Can I set a time limit for my Poll?

Yes, you can incorporate a timer into the questions.

How do I view Poll analytics?

Access detailed analytics for each Poll from your dashboard.
